TCG Tournament Manager 0.2.4 for Windows: run One Piece, Pokémon and Magic: The Gathering
(Modern and Commander) tournaments for a club on one PC, without internet.

## What's new in 0.2.4

### Commander scoring

- New Commander events use the club's scoring: win 5, clear second place 2.5 (still a
  loss), other losses 0, draw 1, bye 5. Half points are shown as 2.5, 7.5 and so on.
- After a win, choose the pod's second place: the player knocked out last, or "No clear
  second" when the last players went out at the same moment. Until that is chosen the
  pod's points are marked provisional, and the round cannot be finalized.
- Draws: the players still in the game each get the full draw points (they are not
  split), and players already knocked out take a loss. The other policy, "Whole pod
  draws", is still available. Every event keeps the draw policy it was created with.

### Optional top-four final

- Tick "Add a top-four final" when setting up a Commander event. After the last
  qualifying round the four best players in the standings play one final pod, seeded and
  seated by the standings.
- The final's page has two sections side by side, or one above the other in a narrow
  window. Under **Record knockout**, select everyone knocked out at the same moment and
  choose **Confirm knockout**. Under **Determine winner**, choose the winner, or **No
  winner / Draw**. The places are shown before **Confirm final results** saves them.
- Places: the first player out is 4th, the next 3rd, and so on, and the winner is 1st.
  Players knocked out together are ordered by qualifying seed among themselves. With no
  winner, the players still in are placed by seed. The final adds no points, and the
  clock never decides the result.
- Once the final has started (its clock started, a knockout recorded, or the result
  confirmed), a correction to a qualifying round can no longer reseed it. This holds even
  if the knockout is undone or the clock reset.
- A confirmed final can be corrected, with a reason.

### Easier result entry and fixes

- Commander results are entered on the pod cards: **Winner +5**, then **2nd +2.5** or **No
  clear second**. Draws are entered on the card too. No window opens for routine results,
  and the page keeps its place.
- Each pod says in words whether it has no result, still needs a second place, is saved
  but provisional, or is final. A save that is refused says why on the card and keeps
  what was entered.
- Changing or clearing a saved result, undoing a knockout and correcting the final all
  ask for a reason. The reason and the values before and after are kept in History.
- Rounds: the suggested number is shown with the number of checked-in players it is
  based on. Rounds you set yourself are no longer replaced when more players check in.
  **Use suggested rounds** goes back to the suggestion.
- Rolling the mouse wheel or a trackpad over a closed drop-down or number field now
  scrolls the page, instead of changing the value.
- The final's page no longer jumps down after a knockout. The last player left cannot be
  knocked out: the page says to confirm the winner instead.

## Install

1. Download `TcgTournamentManager-0.2.4-win64.zip` below.
2. Check its SHA-256 in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\TcgTournamentManager-0.2.4-win64.zip`.
   It must be `3109BF0D00DC0C1BD8FE04DB9D0376728CB58EDA91BE1B794B4F6F92F534A007`.
3. Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, and extract everything. Keep the extracted
   folder together: the program needs the files beside it, so do not run it from inside
   the ZIP or copy out only the .exe.
4. Double-click `TcgTournamentManager.exe` in the extracted folder
   `TcgTournamentManager-0.2.4`.

Nothing else has to be installed. The program is not code-signed, so Windows may show
"Windows protected your PC" the first time. If the checksum matches, choose "More info",
then "Run anyway". The README inside the ZIP is a short guide for organizers.

## Updating from 0.2.3

Your tournaments, players and results are not in the program folder. They are in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\TcgTournamentManager`, and 0.2.4 uses them as they are.

1. Back up first. In 0.2.3, choose Settings > Export database backup..., and save the copy
   on a USB stick or another safe place.
2. Close 0.2.3.
3. Extract the 0.2.4 ZIP into a new folder. Keep the 0.2.3 folder until you are happy
   with 0.2.4.
4. Start 0.2.4. On its first start it updates the database format (one addition, for the
   final), and before doing so it saves a copy in the backups folder.
5. Check that your tournaments and players are there, then delete the old 0.2.3 folder
   if you like.

Commander events created in 0.2.3 keep their rules: second place there is recognition
only, worth no points, and those events cannot add a final. 0.2.3 has not been tested
with a database that 0.2.4 has updated. If you need to go back, use the backup from
step 1.

## Known limitations

- Scoring and tiebreakers are the club's own settings, not certified by Bandai, The
  Pokémon Company or Wizards of the Coast.
- In a Commander event created in 0.2.3, adding a second place later counts as a
  correction and asks for a reason.
- One-on-one matches can only be reported as a win for one player: there is no way to
  enter a draw on the round screen.
- Players cannot be added once a tournament has started.
- English only. One PC and one Windows user; nothing is synced. No installer, automatic
  updates or code signing.
